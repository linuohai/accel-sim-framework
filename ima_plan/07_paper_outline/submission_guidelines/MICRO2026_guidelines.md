# MICRO 2026 Submission Guidelines

> Source: https://www.microarch.org/micro59/submit/guidelines.php
> Last updated: 2026-03-29

## Conference Info

- **Full name**: 59th IEEE/ACM International Symposium on Microarchitecture (MICRO-59)
- **Dates**: October 31 – November 4, 2026
- **Location**: Athens, Greece
- **Website**: https://microarch.org/micro59/

## Important Dates

| Milestone | Date |
|-----------|------|
| Abstract / Paper Registration | March 31, 2026, 11:59 PM EDT |
| Full Paper Submission | April 7, 2026, 11:59 PM EDT |
| Rebuttal / Revision Period | June 3 – 17, 2026 |
| Author Notification | July 7, 2026 |
| Camera-Ready Deadline | September 11, 2026 |

**Submission portal**: https://micro2026.hotcrp.com/

## Page Limits

- **Main content**: maximum **11 pages**, single-spaced, two-column
- **References**: no page limit, but each reference must explicitly list **all authors** (no "et al.")
- **Appendix**: **not allowed**

## Typography

| Element | Size |
|---------|------|
| Body text | 9pt, Times |
| Abstract | 9pt, Times |
| Section headings | 12pt |
| Subsection headings | 10pt |
| Captions | 9pt minimum |
| References | 8pt |
| Line spacing | 11pt |

## Layout

- **Paper size**: US Letter (8.5" x 11")
- **Top / Bottom margins**: 1 in
- **Left / Right margins**: 0.75 in
- **Column gap**: 0.25 in
- **Columns**: 2

## Formatting Restrictions

- **Must use** the official MICRO 2026 LaTeX template (`micro59-latex-template.zip`)
- **Must not** adjust the template or violate its formatting settings
- **Must not** squeeze additional space (e.g., `\vspace`, packages that manipulate vertical space)
- Submissions are **automatically inspected** — violations cause rejection even if HotCRP format check passes
- Figures must be legible and **distinguishable in grayscale**

## Anonymization (Double-Blind Review)

- **No author names** on any submitted documents (except on the submission form)
- **PDF metadata** must not reveal authors
- **Fully anonymize** any links to artifacts (e.g., GitHub repositories)
- **Remove** any links that cannot be fully anonymized
- **No acknowledgments** in the submission
- Include **page numbers** and a **banner at the top of the title page** with submission number
